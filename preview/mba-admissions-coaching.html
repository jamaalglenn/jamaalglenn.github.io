The work

Strategy before polish

I know what it means to approach a dream school from outside the networks where names like Stanford and Harvard are part of dinner-table conversation. My own path was messy and unconventional. It taught me that the best applications are specific, human, and impossible to ignore.

I work one-on-one with a small number of applicants. Together, we identify the through line in your experience, pressure-test your school strategy, and turn raw material into essays and interviews that sound like you on your clearest day.

How coaching works

01

Positioning

Define the candidacy: goals, school fit, strengths, risks, and the story connecting them.

02

03

Interviews

Prepare to answer with substance and ease, from your career choices to the questions you did not expect.
