Speaking

I speak to companies, universities, conferences, and podcasts - strong opinions on AI, money, and work, from someone who runs the numbers for a living.

Jamaal Glenn is a frequent speaker, moderator, and commentator on the business decisions shaping finance, technology, and work. Drawing on his experience as a CFO, investor, entrepreneur, board director, and former NYU and CUNY professor, he speaks about fintech and the future of finance, AI and the future of work, local news, and board governance. He has spoken and moderated for the Council on Foreign Relations, the German Marshall Fund, universities, conferences, and podcasts. He has written for and been featured by TechCrunch, Fortune, TIME, and L'Express.

Jamaal speaks on AI inference economics, AI compensation, corporate AI adoption, AI labor-market effects, and AI board oversight.
