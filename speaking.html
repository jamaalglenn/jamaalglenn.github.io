Speaking

Jamaal Glenn is a frequent speaker, moderator, and commentator on the business decisions shaping finance, technology, and work - and an expert on the economics of AI - what it costs, what it does to work, and how companies and boards should respond. Drawing on his experience as a CFO, investor, entrepreneur, board director, and former NYU and CUNY professor, he speaks about fintech and the future of finance, AI and the future of work, local news, and board governance. He has spoken and moderated for the Council on Foreign Relations, the German Marshall Fund, universities, conferences, and podcasts, and written for and been featured by TechCrunch, Fortune, TIME, and L'Express.
