Jamaal Glenn Admitted to Stanford Graduate School of Business

2011

Jamaal Glenn has been admitted to the Stanford Graduate School of Business, a milestone that opens a new chapter in a career spanning journalism and finance.

Glenn receives the news in a voicemail from admissions dean Derrick Bolton while sitting in his car between consulting meetings in New Jersey. The message arrives after an unconventional application path and years spent building experience outside the networks that often make elite institutions feel attainable.

At Stanford, Glenn plans to deepen his work across entrepreneurship, technology, investing, and media.
