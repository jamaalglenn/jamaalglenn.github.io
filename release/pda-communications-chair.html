Jamaal Glenn Named Chair of the Private Directors Association Communications, Marketing & Editorial Committee

January 2026

Jamaal Glenn has been named chair of the Private Directors Association Communications, Marketing & Editorial Committee, expanding his leadership work at the intersection of corporate governance, editorial strategy, and modern communications.

As chair, Glenn leads the committee's work to help the association translate complex governance expertise into clear, useful content for directors and business leaders. The committee supports how the organization develops, evaluates, and presents ideas across its communications and editorial channels.

Glenn brings experience across board governance, finance, media, marketing, entrepreneurship, and digital strategy to the role. His appointment follows a year of service as a member of the national committee.
