Jamaal Glenn Joins BUILD NYC Local Advisory Board

2017
BUILD NYC

Jamaal Glenn has joined the BUILD NYC Local Advisory Board, supporting an organization that uses entrepreneurship education to help high-school students develop the skills and confidence needed for college and career success.

Glenn brings experience as an entrepreneur, investor, median executive, and university professor. His board service supports BUILD NYC’s strategy, fundraising, and work with young people across New York City.

The appointment reflects Glenn’s interest in entrepreneurship as a tool for opportunity and in helping students build practical experience before entering college or the workforce.
