Jamaal Glenn Featured in The Coffee Chat #112

May 30, 2026

WASHINGTON, D.C. - Jamaal Glenn was featured in The Coffee Chat #112, published by Decks and Diapers.

The conversation examines a career that has moved across investment banking, investing, entrepreneurship, university teaching, writing, and board service. It places those roles in the context of Glenn's work as founder and CEO of JG Holdings and his experience advising organizations on strategy, investments, and governance.

The episode also treats parenthood as part of the story rather than a footnote. Decks and Diapers describes Glenn as a founder, professor, and father of two, consistent with the publication's focus on the careers and family lives of working parents.

The result is a long-form record of the experiences and judgments that shaped Glenn's work, and of the way he has built a portfolio career across institutions rather than a single professional lane.
