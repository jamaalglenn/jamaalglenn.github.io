Jamaal Glenn Selected as a Council on Foreign Relations Term Member

2019

Jamaal Glenn has been selected as a term member of the Council on Foreign Relations, joining a highly selective program for emerging leaders in international affairs.

The term membership brings Glenn into a community of professionals working across government, business, journalism, academia, and civil society. His selection reflects a career increasingly focused on the connections among markets, technology, governance, and international affairs.

Glenn joins CFR while working across investing, entrepreneurship, media, and university teaching.
