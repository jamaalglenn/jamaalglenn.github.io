New Robot Overlords Cites Jamaal Glenn on AI-Token Compensation

AUGUST 27, 2026

NEW YORK - New Robot Overlords cited Jamaal Glenn's analysis in an examination of AI-token allowances as a new feature of technology compensation packages - and of the gap between their advertised value and the value employees actually retain.

The article distinguishes compute access from salary and equity. A token budget can help an engineer work faster, but it does not vest, appreciate, compound, or follow the employee to a future job. That difference matters when employers fold the face value of compute into a headline compensation figure.

The article states that Glenn "has pointed out that token compensation is a clean way to inflate a headline offer number without moving a single dollar of cash or a single share of equity." It uses a hypothetical offer of $375,000 in salary plus a $100,000 token allowance to show why a $475,000 headline can describe instruments with sharply different value to the employee.

New Robot Overlords also connects the compensation question to workforce planning. If companies are willing to spend heavily on compute to multiply individual productivity, the article asks what that means for future engineering headcount, job security, and the distribution of gains from AI adoption.

The article's conclusion is direct: access to compute can be valuable without being employee-owned wealth. Candidates should separate what they can use while employed from what they will keep after they leave.
