Jamaal Glenn to Host Dinner for 2026 Marshall Memorial Fellows

September 25, 2026

WASHINGTON, D.C. - Jamaal Glenn will host members of the German Marshall Fund of the United States' 2026 Marshall Memorial Fellowship cohort during the program's visit to Washington.

GMF selected the fellows through what it describes as a highly competitive application and selection process. The year combines virtual programming with 24 days of immersive travel across the United States and Europe in September and October.

Glenn is a Marshall Memorial Fellow whose work spans finance, technology, investing, and international exchange. The dinner will give the new cohort space to build relationships across disciplines and countries, compare approaches to shared political and economic questions, and continue the program's tradition of dialogue among emerging American and European leaders.

This release announces Glenn's role as a host. It does not imply that he is a member of the 2026 cohort.
