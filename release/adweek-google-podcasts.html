Adweek Publishes Jamaal Glenn on Why Google Podcasts Matters

July 2018
Adweek

NEW YORK - Adweek has published an essay by Jamaal Glenn examining why the launch of Google Podcasts matters for creators, publishers, advertisers, and the wider audio industry.

Glenn argues that Google’s entry can expand podcast discovery by bringing audio more directly into search and the Android ecosystem. He places the launch inside a broader shift in how audiences find and consume spoken-word media.

The essay draws on Glenn’s experience in media, technology, investing, and digital strategy. Glenn is a vice president at Alumni Ventures Group, founder of Glenn Media Group, and an adjunct professor at New York University.
