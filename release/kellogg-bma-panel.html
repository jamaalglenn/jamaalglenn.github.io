Jamaal Glenn Joins Kellogg BMA Conference Panel on the Public-Private Interface

October 2017

EVANSTON, Ill. - Jamaal Glenn joins the “Public-Private Interface” panel today at the 31st Annual Black Management Association Conference at Northwestern University’s Kellogg School of Management.

The panel examines careers and leadership across business, government, philanthropy, and public service. Glenn participates alongside leaders from the Global Strategists Association, the Gates Foundation, and Baxter.

Glenn is a vice president at First Look Media. His perspective reflects a career crossing finance, technology, media, entrepreneurship, and public affairs.

The conference takes place at Kellogg’s Global Hub in Evanston, Illinois.
