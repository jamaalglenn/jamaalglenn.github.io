Jamaal Glenn Discusses AI, Remote Work, and Equity on The Intersection

June 5, 2024

NEW YORK - Jamaal Glenn appeared on The Intersection podcast for a conversation titled “Bridging the Gap: AI, Remote Work, and Equity in the Workplace.”

The episode brought together three changes often discussed separately: the spread of artificial intelligence, the normalization of remote work, and persistent gaps in access to opportunity. Glenn considered how those forces affect employees and organizations, and why the distribution of gains matters as much as the technology itself.

Restart Consulting introduced Glenn as an entrepreneur, professor, writer, and speaker, and highlighted his experience founding and leading an investment holding company that advises on strategy, investment execution, and corporate governance.

The conversation adds an earlier record of themes that continue through Glenn's work: how new tools change power inside organizations, who gets access to opportunity, and what leaders must understand before adopting technology at scale.
