Jamaal Glenn Selected as a Council of Urban Professionals Fellow

2015

Jamaal Glenn has been selected as a Council of Urban Professionals Fellow, joining a leadership program designed to develop and connect diverse professionals across business, government, and civic life.

The fellowship combines leadership development, policy engagement, community service, and access to a network of professionals working to expand representation and influence in major institutions.

Glenn joins the program with a career spanning finance, technology, media, entrepreneurship, and public affairs.
