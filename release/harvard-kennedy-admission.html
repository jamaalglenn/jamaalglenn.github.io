Jamaal Glenn Admitted to Harvard Kennedy School

2012

Jamaal Glenn has been admitted to Harvard Kennedy School, adding a public-policy education to a career rooted in business, finance, technology, and media.

The program offers Glenn a deeper framework for understanding how institutions, markets, government, and public leadership interact. It adds a new dimension to work that crosses the private, public, and nonprofit sectors.

Glenn plans to pursue a Master in Public Administration and bring the school’s policy training into his work as an entrepreneur and media and technology leader.
