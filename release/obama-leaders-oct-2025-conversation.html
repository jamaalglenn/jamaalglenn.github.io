Jamaal joined President Barack Obama and 206 Obama Leaders from around the world

October 2025

Obama Leaders from the 2025-2026 cohort gathered virtually with President Barack Obama, October 20, 2025.

Jamaal Glenn, chief financial officer of SoLo Funds and founder of JG Holdings, joined President Barack Obama for a virtual conversation with 206 Obama Leaders from around the world. Glenn is a member of the Obama Foundation Leaders USA program's 2025-2026 cohort.

The conversation took place over Zoom on October 20, 2025, on the theme of storytelling for democratic reimagination. It was the first of two conversations planned between the cohort and President Obama during the program year.

Glenn chairs the board of directors of Pivot Fund and serves as audit and finance chair of LION. He writes about finance, AI and work at Jamaal's Letter on Substack.
