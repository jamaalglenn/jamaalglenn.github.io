Jamaal Glenn Moderates Equity-Investor Panel at NYU Veterans Future Lab Summit

June 2019

NEW YORK - Jamaal Glenn moderates a panel on early-stage equity investment today at the NYU Veterans Future Lab Summit, bringing together investors to discuss how capital providers evaluate ventures and work with founders.

The discussion examines investor theses, the differences between institutional and personal investment decisions, the signals investors look for, and common tension points between founders and funders. Those issues include control, governance, exit strategy, negotiation, and coachability.

The summit takes place at the SAP Next-Gen NYC Innovation Space in Hudson Yards. The panel gives veteran entrepreneurs practical insight into how equity investors think about new companies and founder relationships.

Glenn draws on experience spanning venture investing, finance, entrepreneurship, and corporate governance to guide the conversation.
