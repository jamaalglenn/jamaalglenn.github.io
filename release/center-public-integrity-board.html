Jamaal Glenn Joins the Center for Public Integrity Board

2024

Jamaal Glenn has joined the board of the Center for Public Integrity, an investigative news organization focused on inequality and abuses of power.

Glenn brings experience across journalism, finance, entrepreneurship, investing, and nonprofit governance. His media background includes founding digital publications, serving as an executive at First Look Media, and supporting community-centered news organizations through board and investment work.

The appointment expands Glenn’s board service focused on strengthening independent journalism and its institutions.
