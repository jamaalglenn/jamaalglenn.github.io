ProFellow Interviews Jamaal Glenn on Building a Multidisciplinary Career Through Fellowships

February 2021

WASHINGTON, D.C. - ProFellow interviewed Jamaal Glenn about how fellowships helped him build a career across finance, investing, education, public policy, and international affairs.

Glenn described fellowships as a way to create sustained, organization-backed relationships with ambitious people outside one's primary field. “Fellowships allow you to meet and develop these deep relationships,” he said, often through the shared work of research, debate, or public problem-solving.

“Some of the most novel and important problems don't fit conveniently into traditional boxes.”

That cross-sector quality matched Glenn's approach to his own career. He explained that no single job could contain his interests, so he deliberately built a portfolio of simultaneous professional experiences in business, academia, politics, and investing.

The interview also addressed global perspective. Glenn said fellowship travel can create access to people and institutions that ordinary tourism or a narrow business trip cannot, and that relationships formed in these programs later produced investments, guest lecturers, and professional introductions.

For applicants, his advice was to be brutally specific about the value they will create: what they will contribute during the fellowship, what work they will produce, and how the sponsoring organization will benefit from being associated with their long-term career.
