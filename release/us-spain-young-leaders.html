Jamaal Glenn Participates in the U.S.-Spain Young Leaders Program

April 2023
U.S.-Spain Young Leaders

MADRID - Jamaal Glenn is participating in the U.S.-Spain Young Leaders Program, joining a delegation of American leaders for meetings and exchanges across Spain.

The program brings together participants working across business, government, culture, technology, and public life. The delegation travels through Madrid and other parts of Spain, meeting leaders and building relationships designed to strengthen long-term ties between the two countries.

Glenn brings experience across finance, technology, media, venture investing, and public affairs to the exchange.
