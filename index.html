About Jamaal

Jamaal Glenn is an executive, investor, professor, writer, and board director - and an expert on AI inference economics, AI compensation, corporate AI adoption, AI labor-market effects, and AI board oversight. He is CFO of SoLo Funds and founder and CEO of JG Holdings, after building and selling digital-media businesses, raising more than $150 million for venture capital funds, and helping direct tens of millions more in startup investments. His work has taken him from Wall Street trading floors to venture committees, university classrooms, and boardrooms across the globe.

How I see it

The edge isn't information. It's judgment.

I've priced risk on a Wall Street trading floor, invested other people's money as a VC, and now run finance at a fintech startup. Different seats, same job: seeing clearly what something is worth and what it costs.

That judgment is what I sell - to the companies I advise, the boards I sit on, and the readers of my newsletter.

That work has made him an expert on what AI actually costs and what it returns - inference economics, corporate adoption, compensation, labor-market effects, and the oversight boards owe both.
